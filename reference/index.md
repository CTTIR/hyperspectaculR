# Package index

## Compositions

Compositions use measured spectral bands and disclose their selections
and display domains. Supported singleton or repeated selections can
reduce to a single-band result.

- [`hsa_mandala()`](https://cttir.github.io/hyperspectaculR/reference/hsa_mandala.md)
  : Radial Spectral Mandala
- [`hsa_spectral_flux()`](https://cttir.github.io/hyperspectaculR/reference/hsa_spectral_flux.md)
  : Cumulative Spectral Change
- [`hsa_fusion()`](https://cttir.github.io/hyperspectaculR/reference/hsa_fusion.md)
  : Multi-Band Spectral Fusion

## Descriptive summaries

Exact spectral density and experimental RMS slope and equal-band
quartiles; descriptive quantities with stated sampling assumptions.

- [`hsa_spectral_density()`](https://cttir.github.io/hyperspectaculR/reference/hsa_spectral_density.md)
  : Spectral Density
- [`hsa_spectral_gradient()`](https://cttir.github.io/hyperspectaculR/reference/hsa_spectral_gradient.md)
  : RMS Spectral Slope
- [`hsa_spectral_quartiles()`](https://cttir.github.io/hyperspectaculR/reference/hsa_spectral_quartiles.md)
